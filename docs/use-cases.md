## How to read the tags

| Tag | Values |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | **Quick Win**: low complexity, value in days, no alignment needed · **High Value**: medium complexity, weeks, one or more teams · **Strategic**: high complexity, months, department or organization |
| **Pillar** | **T** Technical: credible with engineers · **P** Program: the mechanics of delivery · **M** Management: the human layer |
| **Role** | **IC**: individual contributor TPM · **M**: TPM manager or leader · **IC & M**: both |
| **Opportunity** | **Cognitive Overload**: too much information to hold in your head at once. · **Recurring Time Sinks**: something you do every week takes longer than it should. · **Quality Gaps**: mistakes are costly and a second set of eyes helps. · **Decision Support**: you need better data or a clearer risk picture to decide. · **Communication at Scale**: many audiences need the same facts, framed differently. · **Knowledge Capture**: knowledge lives in one head and needs to outlast it. |
| **Stage** | Planning · Executing · Closing · Leadership |

Tags follow the book chapters. Chapter 4's summary tables were printed earlier and differ for a few use cases; where they do, follow the chapter.
