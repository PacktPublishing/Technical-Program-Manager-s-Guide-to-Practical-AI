## Key events

**July 25 — PULSE M4 misses.** FlowEngine at 313/340 (92%). Slips to Aug 7.

The cause is not velocity (INC-2026-0126). The final 27 pipelines shared execution state through Airflow XCom side-channels that the migration runbook never captured. Cutting over a subset left the remainder waiting on state that would only ever be written on the Temporal side, and the scheduler starved. The team rolled back rather than forcing it through, and began tracing dependencies by hand.

The runbook (M2) had been validated against DataCore, whose pipelines are far less entangled than FlowEngine's. The tool built to de-risk the migration had a blind spot exactly where the migration was hardest.

**July 28 — Nuvera asks the direct question.** Rajiv Patel (CDO) raises support case SC-2026-0399: will SOC 2 Type II certification be in hand on August 31? The honest answer under DR-014 is no — readiness Aug 31, report Dec 15. His Q3 board window cannot be met with a readiness statement. Opportunity moves to Q4, probability 80% → 72%.

Notable that this arrived through **support**, not through the account team.
