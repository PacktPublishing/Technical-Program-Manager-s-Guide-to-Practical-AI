Key Tensions from the Debate

Observation period as CONDITIONAL vs. NOT READY trigger. Three advisors initially voted CONDITIONAL. The Skeptic's finding — that the program has never confirmed with the auditor what evidence observation period is required for Type II — changed those votes. If the answer is 6 months, controls completing after February don't qualify, and the certification scope is fundamentally different from the plan.
CTO vs. the field on governance triage. The CTO argued governance gaps (exec mandate, RACI, escalation path) are fixable in 7 days if Yuki and Whitfield prioritize it, and held CONDITIONAL. The Director of TPM and Skeptic argued six weeks of inaction on the exec mandate is structural evidence the dynamic won't change with a deadline alone — and went NOT READY.
Governance fix vs. milestone plan rebuild. The Ops Lead argued that even with perfect governance, the milestone plan is still broken — M4 and M5 in July with zero buffer is a scheduling error, not a governance artifact, and requires a plan rebuild regardless of mandate status.
SHIELD vs. ATLAS circular dependency. The CPO identified that forcing Model Serving to prioritize SHIELD over ATLAS creates a circular dependency: SHIELD unlocks enterprise deals through compliance; ATLAS converts them through NovaAI. Depriving ATLAS of Model Serving capacity to serve SHIELD may undermine the revenue the certification is supposed to enable.
