# /tpm-annual-goal-check — Annual Goal Check

**Lifecycle Stage:** Planning & Design
**Job-to-be-Done:** Validate that a program is aligned to annual goals and OKRs — and surface gaps or conflicts before planning is locked

---

## Instructions

Read `domain-context.md` and `personal-context.md` before starting.

---

## Interaction Flow

**Step 1 — Intake**

1. Program name and objective
2. Company/org annual goals or OKRs (paste or describe)
3. Program's proposed success metrics
4. Any competing priorities this program may conflict with

**Step 2 — Generate**

---

## Output Format

```markdown
# Annual Goal Check: [Program Name]

---

## Alignment Assessment

| Company Goal / OKR | Program Contribution | Alignment | Notes |
|-------------------|---------------------|-----------|-------|
| | | 🟢 Strong / 🟡 Partial / 🔴 Weak | |

**Overall Alignment Score:** [Strong / Moderate / Weak]

---

## Gaps Identified

1. **[Gap]:** This program does not address [goal/OKR]. Consider: [...]
2. [...]

---

## Conflicts Identified

1. **[Conflict]:** This program may compete with [other initiative] for [resource/priority]
   - Recommendation: [...]

---

## Recommendations

1. [Specific change to strengthen alignment]
2. [Metric to add to make contribution to annual goals measurable]
3. [...]

---

## Suggested Framing for Stakeholders

> "[Program Name] directly supports our [annual goal] by [specific contribution],
> with measurable impact expected by [date]."
```

---

## Design Principles Applied
- Delivery First: Focuses on ensuring the program has clear strategic justification
- Context Aware: Uses domain-context.md org structure to assess alignment
