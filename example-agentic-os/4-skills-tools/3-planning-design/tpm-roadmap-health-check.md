# /tpm-roadmap-health-check — Roadmap Health Check

**Lifecycle Stage:** Planning & Design
**Job-to-be-Done:** Assess roadmap clarity, sequencing logic, feasibility, and strategic alignment — catch roadmap problems before they become delivery problems

---

## Instructions

Read `domain-context.md` and `personal-context.md` before starting.

---

## Interaction Flow

**Step 1 — Intake**

1. Roadmap overview (paste or describe: items, owners, dates, phases)
2. Strategic goals this roadmap is meant to serve
3. Team capacity constraints
4. Current quarter and horizon being assessed (e.g., Q2–Q4)

**Step 2 — Generate**

Assess across five dimensions.

---

## Output Format

```markdown
# Roadmap Health Check: [Program/Team Name]

**Assessment Date:** [Date]
**Horizon:** [e.g., Q2–Q4 2024]
**Overall Health:** 🔴 Poor / 🟡 Fair / 🟢 Good

---

## Dimension Scores

| Dimension | Score | Key Finding |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Strategic alignment | 🔴🟡🟢 | |
| Sequencing & dependencies | 🔴🟡🟢 | |
| Feasibility & capacity | 🔴🟡🟢 | |
| Clarity & definition | 🔴🟡🟢 | |
| Stakeholder confidence | 🔴🟡🟢 | |

---

## Findings

### Strategic Alignment
[Are roadmap items traceable to goals? What's missing?]

### Sequencing & Dependencies
[Are items in the right order? Are hard dependencies respected?]

### Feasibility & Capacity
[Is the volume realistic given team size and known commitments?]

### Clarity & Definition
[Are items specific enough to estimate? What's vague?]

### Stakeholder Confidence
[Based on what's known — would key stakeholders trust this roadmap?]

---

## Issues by Severity

**🔴 Must Fix**
- [Issue + recommended fix]

**🟡 Should Fix**
- [Issue + recommended fix]

**🟢 Nice to Fix**
- [Issue + recommendation]

---

## Recommended Actions

1. [Highest priority action]

```

---

## Design Principles Applied
- Delivery First: Assessment focused on delivery viability, not roadmap aesthetics
- Risk Visibility: Surfacing roadmap risks before they become program risks
- Cross-Team Alignment: Dependency sequencing check
