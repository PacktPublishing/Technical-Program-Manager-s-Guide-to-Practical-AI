# /tpm-crossteam-prep — Cross-Team Sync Prep

**Lifecycle Stage:** Execution & Delivery
**Job-to-be-Done:** Prepare agenda, pre-reads, and alignment goals for a cross-team sync meeting

---

## Instructions

Read `domain-context.md` and `personal-context.md` before starting.

---

## Interaction Flow

**Step 1 — Intake**

1. Meeting purpose (alignment / blocker resolution / decision / status sync)
2. Teams attending
3. Open topics to cover
4. Decisions needed from this meeting
5. Any known tensions or conflicts between teams
6. Date and duration

**Step 2 — Generate**

---

## Output Format

```markdown
# Cross-Team Sync Prep: [Meeting Title]

**Date:** [Date] | **Duration:** [X min]
**Teams:** [List]
**Facilitated by:** [TPM name]
**Meeting goal:** [One sentence — what must be true after this meeting]

---

## Pre-Read (send 24 hours before)

### Context
[2–3 sentences: why this meeting is happening now, what's at stake]

### Status Summary
[Brief status of the program/initiative relevant to this meeting]

### Key Topics
1. [Topic] — [What decision or alignment is needed]
2. [...]

---

## Agenda

| Time | Topic | Owner | Goal (Decide / Align / Inform) |
|------|-------|-------|-------------------------------|
| 0:00 | Welcome + context | [TPM] | Align |
| 0:05 | [Topic 1] | [Owner] | Decide: [specific decision] |
| 0:20 | [Topic 2] | [Owner] | Align: [specific alignment] |
| 0:35 | Blockers & next steps | [TPM] | Decide |
| 0:50 | Wrap + action items | [TPM] | — |

---

## Decisions Needed

| # | Decision | Options | Owner | Must Decide By |
|---|---------|--------|-------|---------------|
| 1 | | | | End of meeting |

---

## Known Tensions to Manage

| Tension | Teams Involved | TPM Approach |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| [e.g., Team A wants X, Team B wants Y] | | [Neutral framing / Pre-align with both before meeting] |

---

## Success Criteria

This meeting was successful if:
- [ ] [Decision 1] was made
- [ ] [Blocker] has a clear owner and timeline
- [ ] All teams leave with the same understanding of [key topic]
```

---

## Design Principles Applied
- Cross-Team Alignment: Explicit decisions, tensions, and success criteria
- Delivery First: Agenda designed for decisions, not status theater
