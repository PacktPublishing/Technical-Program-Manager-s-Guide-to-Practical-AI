# Career direction statement: Jordan (fictional sample)

**In two to three years** I want to be a Staff TPM leading a platform or infrastructure program that several product teams depend on, or a TPM manager with a small team. I am not sure which yet.

**More of:** architecture conversations, cross-org programs, mentoring newer TPMs.
**Less of:** status reporting by hand, being the default owner of anything that has no owner.

**Constraints:** I want to stay remote or hybrid with at most two office days. I do not want a role with a regular on-call rotation while my kids are small. I would take a lateral move for the right scope, but not a pay cut.

**Still unsure about:** whether I want to manage people or stay on the IC path, and whether a startup would suit me better than a large company.
