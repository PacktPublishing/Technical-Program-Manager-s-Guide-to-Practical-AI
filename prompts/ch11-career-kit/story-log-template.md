# Story log

One entry per story. Keep the same fields every time. Write the entries yourself, or let AI organize your spoken notes (P11.1 and P11.2), then check every field against your memory and redact anything confidential.

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Date | Month and year |
| Situation | The context in one or two sentences |
| Action | What you did, in your own words |
| Result | The outcome, in numbers or concrete consequences |
| Lesson | What you would tell yourself next time |
| Skills and themes | Two or three tags |
| Growth area | What this story shows you still need to work on |
