# Halden FY2026 Engineering OKRs (excerpt)

**Objective 3: Simplify the payments stack**
*Owner: Marcus Webb, VP Engineering*

| Key result | Owner | Status |
|------------|-------|--------|
| KR 3.1 — Consolidate payment platforms | Marcus Webb | Not started |
| KR 3.2 — Reduce payments operating cost | Priya Raghavan | Target to be set in Q2 planning |
| KR 3.3 — One on-call rotation for payments | Tom Delacroix | Not started |

*Rationale (from the FY2026 planning doc):* Three platforms mean three processor contracts, three
on-call rotations and three sets of reconciliation jobs. The engineering cost of keeping all three
alive is crowding out roadmap work.
