# Interview notes — Lena Fischer, Director of Product, Payments

Wed 11 Mar 2026 · 50 min · her desk · my notes, quotes marked

- Enthusiastic. "This is the program I've wanted for two years."
- Merchant experience: wants zero change for merchants "unless it's an improvement." No new
  checkout UI in scope as far as she is concerned.
- Sequencing: "We move the Kestrel merchants first. They're the smallest group and the platform we
  most want gone." Marketplace second, then the Halden Pay long tail.
- Timing: "We start switching merchants in May." Thinks a pilot of ~20 Kestrel merchants in May is
  realistic if Platform has the tooling.
- Definition of done: "Done is when the last merchant is switched over. Decommissioning is cleanup
  we can do in Q1." Does not want decommissioning on the critical path.
- Enterprise accounts: assumes they move with everyone else; "they'll want the better platform."
- Payment methods: two local EU payment methods exist only on Kestrel. "Northwind supports both,
  I checked." (Did not say where she checked.)
- Worried about merchant churn if the switch causes payout delays. Wants Merchant Success involved
  early.
- Happy for Product to own merchant communications.
