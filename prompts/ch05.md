## P5.1 · Program charter from mandate

**Section:** Scaffolding Program Infrastructure · **Type:** Full prompt (trimmed in print)  
**Paste with it:** VP mandate email, parent OKR, strategy deck  
**In this repo:** Complete version and sample run: [ch05-payments-consolidation/1-charter/](ch05-payments-consolidation/1-charter/prompt.md)

```
[ROLE] Senior TPM drafting a program charter.
[TASK] From the source material, produce two artifacts:
  1. A draft charter, in our standard section order.
  2. An inference log: every place the source was thin and you
     filled the gap.
[CONTEXT] Attached: VP mandate email, parent OKR, Q2 strategy deck.
[FORMAT] Artifact 2 as a table with columns:
  Charter section | What I inferred | What I inferred it from |
  Confidence (High/Med/Low) | Who can confirm this
[CONSTRAINTS]
- Mark every inference [INFERRED] inside the charter itself.
- Invent no success metric containing a number. Write "target TBD".
- If a section has no support in the source, leave it empty and log
  it. An empty section is more useful to me than a plausible one.
[FEEDBACK LOOP] Before drafting, ask your three sharpest questions.
```

## P5.2 · The alignment gap detector

**Section:** Aligning Stakeholders from the Start · **Type:** Full prompt (trimmed in print)  
**Paste with it:** All stakeholder interview notes  
**In this repo:** Complete version: [ch05-payments-consolidation/2-alignment/](ch05-payments-consolidation/2-alignment/prompt.md) · in the example OS: agent `/tpm-alignment-gap-detector`

```
[ROLE] You reconcile conflicting accounts. You are not a summarizer.
[TASK] From these nine sets of stakeholder notes, find every place
  where two or more people state or imply expectations that cannot
  both be true.
[CONTEXT] Domains, in priority order: scope, timeline and sequencing,
  ownership and decision rights, definition of "done." A conflict
  counts even if the two never discussed the same topic.
[FORMAT] Table: Conflict | Stakeholder A + verbatim quote |
  Stakeholder B + verbatim quote | Why both cannot hold | Domain
[CONSTRAINTS]
- Every row carries a verbatim quote from each side. No quote, no row.
- Do not resolve conflicts or say who is right.
- Preserve hedges exactly. Never harden "probably" into a commitment.
- Below the table, list separately every position stated by exactly
  one person that nobody else addressed.
```

> The Oct 25 proof prints "expectations that cannot be true". This repo keeps "cannot both be true", which matches the "Why both cannot hold" column; a correction to the proof has been requested.

## P5.3 · The assumption extractor

**Section:** Scoping What You Don't Know Yet · **Type:** Full prompt (trimmed in print)  
**Paste with it:** The approved scope document  
**In this repo:** Complete version: [ch05-payments-consolidation/3-assumptions/](ch05-payments-consolidation/3-assumptions/prompt.md) · in the example OS: agent `/tpm-assumption-extractor`

```
[ROLE] A skeptical reviewer reading this scope document
  adversarially, with no stake in it being approved.
[TASK] Extract every claim the document treats as settled without
  stating evidence for it.
[CONTEXT] Categories: capacity, sequencing, definitions, external
  parties, technical.
[FORMAT] Table: Verbatim sentence | What it assumes | Category |
  If this is wrong, what breaks | Blast radius (High/Med/Low)
[CONSTRAINTS]
- Column one must be an exact quote, character for character. If you
  cannot produce the quote, do not produce the row. Not negotiable.
- Do not extract assumptions about topics the document never
  mentions. Silence is not an assumption.
- One assumption per row.
- Aim for coverage. 30 rows is a normal result here.
```

## P5.4 · Multi-document dependency synthesis (stage 2)

**Section:** Technical Dependency and Architecture Synthesizing · **Type:** Full prompt (trimmed in print)  
**Paste with it:** Stage-1 extractions of every design doc, ownership map  
**In this repo:** Complete version, with stage 1: [ch05-payments-consolidation/4-dependencies/](ch05-payments-consolidation/4-dependencies/prompt-stage2-synthesize.md)

> Stage 1 is Daniella's single-document extraction from Chapter 3 ([P3.2](ch03.md#p32--daniellas-dependency-extraction-one-shot-rtcfc)), run once per document.

```
[ROLE] A systems architect reconciling dependency extractions taken
  independently from 11 design documents.
[TASK] Merge them into one graph, then produce three lists.
[CONTEXT] 4 teams, 18 months, different names for the same
  systems. Ownership map attached. "payments-core", "the core
  payments service" and "PCS" are one node.
[FORMAT] First the alias table you used: Canonical | Aliases | Docs.
  Then: 1. UNOWNED  2. CONFLICTING (quote both)  3. CIRCULAR (show
  the full chain).
[CONSTRAINTS]
- Do not merge two names without a reason. When unsure, keep them
  separate and add a fourth list, POSSIBLE ALIASES, for a human.
- Every claim traces to a document and, where available, a section.
- Do not infer a dependency no document states. A missing edge beats
  an invented one: this goes to an architecture review.
```

## P5.4b · From map to milestones: back-scheduling

**Section:** Technical Dependency and Architecture Synthesizing · **Type:** Follow-up prompt, same conversation as P5.4  
**Paste with it:** The validated dependency map, the charter, the assumption log  
**In this repo:** Sample run: [ch05-payments-consolidation/4-dependencies/output-milestones.md](ch05-payments-consolidation/4-dependencies/output-milestones.md)  
**Expect:** 6 to 8 milestones, the critical path and the float in weeks. In the book's scenario the float drops from ten weeks to one once the real eleven-week legal review is used.

```
Using the validated map, the charter, and the assumption log, back-schedule to 30 September. List 6-8 milestones with: depends on, duration and the artifact row it came from, latest finish, and gate owner. Then give the critical path and the float in weeks. Where two durations conflict, plan on the longer and show both. Do not shorten anything to make the date fit.
```

Take each gate to its owner with one question: can you commit to this date?

## P5.5 · Persona-swarm pre-mortem - SRE lens

**Section:** Pre-Mortem on Demand · **Type:** Full prompt (trimmed in print)  
**Paste with it:** Charter, conflict register, assumption log, validated dependency map, milestone skeleton  
**In this repo:** All four lenses: [ch05-payments-consolidation/5-premortem/](ch05-payments-consolidation/5-premortem/README.md) · in the example OS: skill `/tpm-premortem`

> The book prints the SRE lens; the other three (security, staff engineer, product) use the same structure.

```
[ROLE] An SRE with twelve years operating payment systems, paged at
  3am for other people's migrations. Not this program's TPM, and no
  stake in it succeeding.
[TASK] It is six months from now and this program failed. Write the
  incident narratives, from your lens only.
[CONTEXT] Full corpus attached: charter, conflict register,
  assumption log, validated dependency map, and milestone skeleton.
[FORMAT] Three to five narratives. Each: Title | What happened, in
  sequence | Earliest observable signal | Which document should have
  caught this and did not
[CONSTRAINTS]
- Stay inside your lens. Operational and reliability failures only.
- Ground every narrative in the corpus and cite it.
- Do not hedge. This failure already happened.
- Rank your own narratives by likelihood and say why.
```
