# 20 — Hard conversation prep: TPM-R and the 5-day escalation gap

**Source:** fixtures/people/hard-conversation-notes.md. No script, as instructed.

## What they may hear that you don't intend

- "Program 2 went Red because of you." The notes say "partly because of it". If you name the Red status first, they'll hear blame for the whole outcome.
- "Your team failed." They hear criticism of the team as criticism of themselves.
- "Your flow inventory praise last week wasn't real." Contrast with recent praise can read as a set-up.

## Where they'll likely get defensive

- **The 5 days (10-09 → 10-14):** they may defend it as time spent preparing options. Their pattern is to over-prepare.
- **Escalation itself:** they've told you escalating "feels like admitting failure". The core message touches that belief directly.

## What to lead with

- The Staff goal they care about, and that escalation timing is now the main open gap for it (S3).
- That an early flag, even without a full plan, is what you want from them.

## Watch for

They go quiet and over-prepare next time. Agree on a concrete threshold together, for example "flag within 48 hours, options optional", so that quiet doesn't turn into a longer delay.
