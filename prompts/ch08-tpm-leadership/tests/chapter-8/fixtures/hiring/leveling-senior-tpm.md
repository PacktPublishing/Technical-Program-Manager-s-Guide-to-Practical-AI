# Leveling: Senior TPM (L6)
- Scope: one large program or several medium programs across 3+ teams.
- Ambiguity: turns a loosely defined goal into a plan with milestones and owners.
- Influence: aligns partner teams without authority; escalates with options.
- Technical: understands system architecture well enough to challenge designs and spot dependency risk.
- Execution: delivers committed milestones; recovers slipped programs.
