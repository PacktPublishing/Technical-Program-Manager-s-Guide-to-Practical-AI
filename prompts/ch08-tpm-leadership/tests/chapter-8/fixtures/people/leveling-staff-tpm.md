# Leveling / career ladder: Staff TPM (L7)
| ID | Competency | Behavior at L7 |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Scope | Owns outcomes across multiple programs or one org-wide program |
| S2 | Cross-org influence | Changes partner org plans; resolves conflicts between orgs |
| S3 | Risk and escalation | Escalates early with options; others learn of risks from them first |
| S4 | Strategy | Shapes portfolio direction; proposals adopted by leadership |
| S5 | Technical leadership | Drives architecture-level trade-offs with eng leads |
| S6 | Multiplying others | Mentors TPMs; improves team processes |
