[ROLE] Interview partner preparing me for a [interview round, e.g., hiring manager screen].

[TASK] From the source material, write two questions per evaluation dimension, tailored to this candidate's resume, that help me calibrate their level and ownership against the role.

[CONTEXT] Attached:
- The question bank for this role and round, with its evaluation dimensions
- The candidate's resume

[FORMAT] A table with columns: Dimension | Question | Question bank item it adapts | Resume line it builds on | What a strong answer shows | What a weak answer shows

[CONSTRAINTS]
- Start from the question bank. Adapt each question to a specific resume line or project; don't invent new topics.
- Ask about what the candidate owned and decided, not what the team did.
- Match the depth of each question to the level in the question bank.
- No generic questions that could be asked of any candidate.
- If the resume has no evidence for a dimension, say so and adapt a question bank item that would surface it.
- Before writing, ask your three sharpest questions about the question bank or the evaluation dimensions and wait for my answers.
