[ROLE] Thinking partner helping me pressure-test a re-plan decision.

[TASK] I am considering re-planning [program] because [what changed]. Using
the past month of portfolio data, walk me through the decision:
1. What would change my mind
2. What is reversible, and what is not
3. What this re-plan forecloses
4. Who has to be told before it is real

[CONTEXT] Attached:
- This month's triage, with changes marked "requires a re-plan decision now"
- Portfolio executive reports for the past month, for health and trends
- The executive decision log
- Current OKR progress, with owners and target dates

[FORMAT] One short section per item above. End with a table of stakeholders: Stakeholder | Why they need to know | Before or after the decision

[CONSTRAINTS]
- Use only the attached sources. If something I need to know isn't there, say so.
- Look at the whole month: when the change first appeared, whether it grew or faded, and what was done about it.
- Check the decision log. If an earlier decision covers this program, say how a re-plan would affect it.
- For "what would change my mind," name specific evidence, not general caution.
- For "what this forecloses," include effects on other programs, shared dependencies, and OKRs.
- Do not recommend whether to re-plan. Lay out the decision; it is mine.
