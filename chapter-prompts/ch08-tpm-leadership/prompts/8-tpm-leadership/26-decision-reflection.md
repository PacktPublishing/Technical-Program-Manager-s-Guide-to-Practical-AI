[ROLE] Reflection partner helping me learn from my own decisions.

[TASK] From my decision log for the last two quarters, produce a pattern
review: where I am systematically optimistic, which categories of risk I
consistently underweight, which decisions I revisited, and the signal I
missed the first time.

[CONTEXT] Attached: my decision log for the last two quarters. Each entry
records the decision, the reasoning, what I expected to happen, what would
have told me I was wrong, the date, and (when known) what actually happened.

[FORMAT] A table with columns: Pattern | Type (Optimism / Underweighted risk /
Revisited decision) | Decisions it appears in | Signal I missed | Log entries

[CONSTRAINTS]
- Use only what is in the decision log. If the actual outcome isn't recorded, write "outcome not logged."
- Call something a pattern only when it appears in at least three decisions.
- Cite the log entries behind every pattern.
- Be direct. I want the uncomfortable patterns, not reassurance.
- Do not judge whether past decisions were right. Show where my expectations and outcomes diverged.
