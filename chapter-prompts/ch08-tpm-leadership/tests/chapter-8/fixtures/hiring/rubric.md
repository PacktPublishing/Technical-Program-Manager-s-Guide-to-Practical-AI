# Screening rubric — Senior TPM, DR program (scale 1–4)
| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| R1 Resilience programs | Ran a program with failover, DR, or regional-failure constraints |
| R2 Cross-org influence | Delivered through teams outside their reporting line, incl. security/compliance |
| R3 Technical depth | Can engage on distributed systems, replication, or infrastructure design |
| R4 Execution under slip | Recovered or re-planned a slipping program with evidence of outcome |
## Hiring guide
- Execution (R4) and Influence (R2) weigh highest in the final loop.
- Loop: HM screen, technical depth, cross-functional partner, bar raiser.
