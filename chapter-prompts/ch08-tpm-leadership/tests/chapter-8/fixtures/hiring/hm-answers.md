# Hiring manager answers (test answers to the model's clarifying questions)
- Hands-on DR: running a failover test end to end is required; designing replication is not (engineers own it).
- On-call: the role joins the SRE escalation rotation for DR events only, about one week per quarter.
- Compliance: has taken a program through an audit before is preferred, not required; Compliance team coaches.
- Location/level: level is Senior TPM (L6). Years of experience: we don't use a threshold.
- Rubric scale: 1–4 (1 = no signal, 2 = some, 3 = meets L6, 4 = above L6). "No evidence" is different from 1.
- Dimensions weigh equally; the rubric is for screening only, not the final decision.
- Interview round for question prep: hiring manager screen (45 min), L6 depth.
- Finalist comparison: Execution and Influence weigh highest in the hiring guide; notes come from 4 interviewers.
